# 📣 Creator track: bring in real users (ref to earn)

*Updated 2026-11-13. Rules from the "referral programme" FAQ on [docs.flop.network](https://docs.flop.network).*

## How creators are paid

Funding comes from the **800M $FLOP ecosystem fund**. There are no special KOL deals: no paid posts, no fixed salary, no reserved allocation.
Every creator follows the same rules:

- **Sign-up:** verified email + 1 $FLOP wallet + 1 or more social accounts (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Naver).
  Each account is verified by posting from that account.
- **Personal ref link:** every wallet created through your link is credited to you. **One tier only**, no multi-level.
- **Public leaderboard** on the explorer. During testnet it ranks by $FLOP that **your referred wallets** spent on inference,
  received from inference, and spent via HTLC (swaps). At mainnet it ranks by the change in their $FLOP holdings.
- **Rewards each period:** the top creators receive a share proportional to their community.
- **Lottery:** each period, some wallets registered through ref links are drawn at random and split the prize pool. Invitees benefit too.
- **Not counted:** views, likes, clicks, because those can be faked. Validators cannot be referred.

The old scheme (6–10% commission on miner income, 10% on agent spend) **has been dropped**.

## Who it suits

People with a real community who can get them to **do real work**: run agents, spend FLOP on inference, run miners.
A community that only farms the faucet and leaves will not rank.
